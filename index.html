About NEXUS

Not another hackathon.

NEXUS 0 — THE INNOVATION ARENA is a competitive innovation challenge designed to test students’ technical knowledge, creativity, communication, teamwork, strategic thinking, and ability to perform under time pressure. Instead of simply picking a problem and building a project, every team is split into two specialized subgroups.

Technical Team

Responsible for coding, development, and solving technical challenges.

Pitching Team

Responsible for understanding problems, developing solutions, presenting ideas, and managing communication.

Pitch → Coins

Initial pitches earn points. Those points become virtual currency, spent strategically on coding challenges in the auction.

Surprise challenge

An unexpected technical challenge, plus a live leaderboard, with a final evaluation to conclude.

Defend your idea

Teams must show technical ability and the ability to communicate and defend their ideas.

Competition

Five phases. One arena.

Rounds are numbered as confirmed by the organizers.

Team Structure

Four or five members. Two subgroups.

Pick a team size (minimum 4, maximum 5), then tap a subgroup or the seats to try a split. Every team needs at least two in each.

Team allocation

Teams choose a size of 4 or 5 and decide their own distribution: 2 technical + 2 pitching for four members, or 2 + 3 / 3 + 2 for five. The fifth member, if any, is assigned by the team’s strengths. All members can collaborate during planning and the final presentation.

Cross-branch & cross-year teams welcome

Students can select team members from different engineering branches and different academic years. Teams do not need to be from the same branch or year. NEXUS 0 encourages interdisciplinary collaboration, and registration is not restricted by branch or year.

Rounds

Round 1 — The Problem Statement Challenge

Objective

To test the team’s ability to understand real-world problems and identify practical solutions.

  • Organizers prepare problem statements across sectors and themes.
  • Each team selects a problem statement from the theme selected by the organizers.
  • Preparation time: approximately 15–20 minutes, with all team members contributing.
  • After preparation, only Pitching Team members present to the judges.

Round 2 — The NEXUS Coding Challenge Auction

Objective

To introduce strategic decision-making into the technical competition. Instead of giving every team the same challenge, organizers run a live auction where teams compete to acquire coding challenges using the NEXUS Coins earned in Round 1.

  • NEXUS Coins balance carries over from Round 1.
  • Coding challenge cards are shown as they are revealed.
  • Strategic decisions: which challenge to acquire, and at what cost.
  • Technical Team members are present during the auction, but the auction itself is done by Pitching Team members only.
